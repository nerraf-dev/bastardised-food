

## Bastardised Chicken & Veg Curry

This recipe is a testament to the "use what you have" philosophy. It started with a glut of veg from the market—courgette, aubergine, squash—and a single tin of tomatoes. The result is a comforting, vegetable-packed curry that’s a little bit Indian, a little bit Mediterranean, and a lot delicious. It’s perfect for a big pot on a chilly evening, with plenty left over for the freezer.

**The Ingredients**
* About 1.2kg of mixed vegetables: **calabaza** (squash), **calbacín** (courgette), **berenjena** (aubergine), **red onion**, and **pimento rojo** (red pepper). I also added a **potato** and some **broccoli**.
* 260g of **chicken thighs**, cubed
* 1 tin of **chopped tomatoes**
* 1 tin of **coconut milk**
* **Spices:** Cumin seeds, ground cumin, and dried bay leaves
* **Fresh herbs:** Parsley and coriander (cilantro)
* Olive oil and salt

**The Method**

1.  **Prep Your Veg:** Chop all your vegetables into generous, bite-sized cubes. Chop the chicken into similar-sized pieces. The bigger chunks mean the vegetables hold their shape better during cooking.

2.  **Build the Base:** In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat a good glug of olive oil. Add the chopped **red onion** and cook until it's soft and translucent. Add your **ground cumin** and **cumin seeds** and let them toast in the hot oil for about a minute.

3.  **Add the Tough Stuff:** Stir in the cubed **squash**, **potato**, and **aubergine**. These are the heartiest vegetables and need the longest to cook. Toss in the **bay leaves** and give it all a good stir to coat the vegetables in the spices.

4.  **Introduce the Liquid:** Pour in the tin of **chopped tomatoes** and the can of **coconut milk**. Add the **chicken thighs** and a good pinch of salt. Give it a stir and bring the whole pot to a gentle simmer.

5.  **Let It All Mingle:** Lower the heat and let the curry simmer for at least 30 minutes. This is where the magic happens. The squash and potato will get wonderfully tender, and the flavors will meld together into a rich, complex sauce.

6.  **Add the Final Touches:** Stir in the **courgette** and **broccoli** and continue to cook for another 5-10 minutes, or until they're just tender. Remove the bay leaves.

7.  **Finish Strong:** Turn off the heat and stir in a big handful of chopped **fresh parsley** and **coriander**. Serve it hot over rice or with some crusty bread for dipping.

This dish proves that "bastardised" can mean something truly delicious and unique. Enjoy!

### Vegetable & Chicken Curry (with updated liquid and herbs)

1.  **Sauté the Aromatics and Spices:** In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat a generous splash of oil over medium heat. Add the **red onions** and cook until soft, about 5-7 minutes. Push the onions to one side, then add your spices to the hot oil. Use 2 teaspoons of ground **cumin** and toast them for a minute until fragrant. Add the **cumin seeds** now as well for a nutty crunch. Stir everything together.

2.  **Add the Tougher Vegetables, Bay Leaves, and Tomatoes:** Add the cubed **squash**, **aubergine**, and **potato** to the pot. Add the **dried bay leaves** right in the pot. Stir in the **tin of chopped tomatoes** and the **chicken thighs**.

3.  **Introduce the Coconut Milk:** Pour in the can of **coconut milk**. The tomatoes will add a lot of liquid, so you may not need any extra water. Bring the mixture to a simmer.

4.  **Simmer and Cook Down:** Lower the heat and let the curry gently simmer for at least 20-30 minutes, or until the squash and potato are tender. This is where all the flavors will meld together and the sauce will begin to thicken.

5.  **Add the Softer Vegetables:** Stir in the **courgette** and **broccoli** and continue to cook for another 5-10 minutes, or until they are cooked through but still have a bit of bite.

6.  **Final Adjustments and Fresh Herbs:** Remove the bay leaves. Taste the curry and adjust the seasoning as needed. Stir in a large handful of chopped **fresh parsley** and **coriander**.

Enjoy your delicious and now even richer homemade curry!
